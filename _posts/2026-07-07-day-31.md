what_i_learned: |
   Today's session focused on strengthening both my machine learning knowledge and the dataset analysis supporting our DermaBridge project. I completed the remaining modules from the Google Machine Learning Crash Course assigned by my graduate mentor. I covered several topics such as working with categorical data, dataset characteristics, labels, imbalanced datasets, data transformation, dataset splitting, generalization, overfitting, model complexity, L2 regularization, and interpreting loss curves. These lessons gave me a much better understanding of what makes a high quality dataset and how factors such as class imbalance, poor labeling, and overfitting can impact a model’s ability to perform well on unseen data. I also learned how techniques like L2 regularization can help improve a model’s ability to generalize, while training and validation loss curves can be used to evaluate model performance and detect overfitting. In addition to my coursework, I continued working on the Individual Typology Angle (ITA) analysis for one of our skin lesion datasets. Although I was not able to complete the analysis, I spent time working through the methodology and troubleshooting parts of the implementation that I found challenging. While the process was more difficult than I expected, it helped me develop a better understanding of how ITA is used to estimate skin tone and why it is important for evaluating the diversity and fairness of medical imaging datasets. Overall, I made meaningful progress in both my understanding of machine learning concepts and my ability to contribute to the technical aspects of our research project.
