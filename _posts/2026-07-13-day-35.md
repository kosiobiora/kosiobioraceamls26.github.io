   Today's session focused on advancing the mobile application development phase of our DermaBridge project while continuing to prepare for our upcoming pitch video. I began working on the user interface of the application, exploring different design approaches and drawing inspiration from existing platforms to identify features and layouts that could improve the overall user experience. As a team, we discussed the essential functionalities we wanted to include and began designing an interface that would be intuitive, accessible, and aligned with the goals of our project. We also developed several key pages, including the landing page and the sign-in/sign-up pages, which helped establish the foundation of the application’s structure and user flow. Beyond the initial design process, we began researching methods for incorporating explainability techniques such as Grad-CAM and SHAP into the camera screening feature of the app. This research focused on exploring how we could provide users with visual explanations of the AI model’s predictions, making the screening process more transparent and easier to understand. We also considered how these explainability features could improve user trust and demonstrate the reasoning behind the model’s outputs. Ahead of filming our pitch video, we prepared the necessary props and finalized the shooting location to ensure the filming process would be organized and efficient. We ended the day by reviewing our plans for the video and making final adjustments to ensure we could effectively communicate the purpose, impact, and innovation behind DermaBridge.
